# Angvey Brand Guide v1.0

## Brand Story

**Angvey** (अंग्वे) — from *Anga* (limb/part, as in *Anga-veda*, the knowledge of the
body) + *Vey* (way). "The way of learning that speaks your language."

Millions of Bharat's brightest minds sit in tier-2/3 classrooms understanding physics
perfectly in Hinglish — then lose marks because every tool that could answer their doubts
speaks only English. Angvey exists so that **no student's rank depends on their English**.

## Positioning

> **Angvey — India's vernacular AI study saathi.**
> हर doubt का जवाब, अपनी भाषा में।
> Voice-first doubt solving + PYQ practice + revision planning for JEE, NEET & UPSC.

One-line pitch: *"Padhai jo tumhari bhasha mein bolti hai"* — studying that speaks your language.

## Personality

| Trait | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Saathi (companion)** | Never condescending. Explains like a senior who cracked the exam last year |
| **Sharp** | NCERT-accurate, step-by-step, exam-focused — no fluff |
| **Warm** | Hinglish by default, encouragement after wrong answers |
| **Bharat-first** | Built for ₹8,000 Android phones and 3G networks, not MacBooks and fiber |

## Voice & Tone

- Default language: **Hinglish** (Hindi structure, English technical terms — how students actually think)
- Formal Hindi for UPSC/GK content; simple English on request
- Sample: *"Arre ye to classic NCERT concept hai! Dekh, Newton ka second law F = ma hai. Ab step-by-step samjhaata hoon..."*

## Logo

- **Icon:** a diya (Indian oil lamp) whose flame rises as a spark — knowledge that grows,
  lit in your own language; the flame doubles as a speech-bubble hint (voice-first).
- **Wordmark:** ANGVEY — bold geometric sans-serif capitals, white.
- **Colors:** deep indigo background (#3B2A8C), saffron→amber flame gradient
  (#FF9933 → #FFD166) — the colors of Indian identity, energy and night-lamp study sessions.
- Files: `brand/logo.png` (master), favicon variants in `web/public/`.

## Typography

- **Headings / wordmark:** "Poppins" (Google Fonts) — geometric, modern, confident
- **Body / UI:** "Inter" (Google Fonts) — clean at small sizes
- **Devanagari (Hindi):** system fallback + "Noto Sans Devanagari" via Google Fonts

## Naming Conventions

- Product surfaces: **Doubt Saathi** (chat), **Practice Arena**, **Revision Planner**, **Dash** (dashboard)
- Plans: **Sahaj** (free), **Sahaj+** (₹49), **Pro** (₹99), **Paathshala** (B2B)
- The assistant persona: **"Vey"** — students talk *to* Vey.

## Do / Don't

- ✅ Do mix Hindi and English naturally (Hinglish)
- ✅ Do use ₹ pricing, exam names (JEE Main/Advanced, NEET, UPSC CSE), real Indian contexts
- ❌ Don't use Western idioms ("piece of cake"), USD pricing, or English-only marketing
- ❌ Don't promise ranks — promise *clarity, speed and confidence*
