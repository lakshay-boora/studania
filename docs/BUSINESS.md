| | |

## 2. Problem → Solution

**Problem:** 25M+ competitive-exam aspirants; ~90% of India doesn't speak English fluently;
existing AI tools are English-first, text-first, ₹500+/month, and don't follow NCERT phrasing
(a mark-costing issue in NEET).

**Solution:** Angvey — a voice-first, Hinglish-native AI doubt-solver grounded in
NCERT-accurate retrieval, with PYQ practice and spaced-repetition revision, priced at ₹49.

## 3. Revenue Streams

| # | Stream | Pricing | Year-1 share | Year-3 share |

Freemium funnel: Sahaj free tier (10 doubts/day) is the acquisition engine — every share
card ("Vey ne bataaya!") is organic distribution on WhatsApp.

## 4. Unit Economics (why open-source wins)

| Cost item | Closed-API competitor | Angvey (open-source) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inference per 1M tokens | $0.15–$3.00 | $0.0003–$0.013 self-hosted |
| 80% of doubts (Tier-1 retrieval) | still full LLM cost | **~₹0** |
| Monthly cost per active user (30 doubts, 20% LLM) | ~₹25–80 | **~₹3–8** |
| Gross margin at ₹49 | negative–30% | **70–85%** |

Break-even: ~₹41L ARR covers a 6-person team in Sonipat with a 2-GPU serving box.

## 5. GTM (Go To Market)

**Phase 1 (months 0–6): Sonipat & Haryana.** Onboard 10 coaching institutes to Paathshala
(free pilot → paid), run "Doubt Marathon" WhatsApp groups, 3 Hinglish YouTube Shorts/week.
Target: 50K installs, 2K paying.

**Phase 2 (months 6–18): NCR + Hindi belt** (UP, Rajasthan, MP, Bihar) — the 400M-strong
Hindi belt where Hinglish is the operating language. Target: 500K installs, 30K paying.

**Phase 3 (year 2+): Pan-India vernacular** — add 8 Indic languages using AI4Bharat/Bhashini
models; state-PSC exam modules.

## 6. Competition & Moat

| Competitor | Their model | Angvey's edge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT/Gemini free | Generic English chatbot | NCERT grounding, Hinglish voice, practice loop, ₹49 tier with offline retrieval |
| PhysicsWallah Alakh AI | Locked in PW ecosystem | Agnostic, 20× cheaper, voice-first |
| Arivihan | AI courses (Tamil-first) | Doubt-first not course-first; Hindi belt focus |
| Edza AI | Premium AI tutors | Mass-market price via open-source cost base |

**Moats:** (1) bilingual NCERT-accurate corpus (compounds daily), (2) Hinglish voice UX tuned
on real Haryanvi/UP accents, (3) cost structure competitors can't follow without abandoning
their API stack, (4) institute relationships.

## 7. Team Plan (lean)

## 8. Funding Path

- **Bootstrapped → IndiaAI Mission grants** (subsidised GPU compute at ₹115–150/hr, ~40%
  project subsidy) + Bhashini free APIs keep burn minimal.
- **Seed (₹2–4Cr) at ~₹40L ARR** for team + 2-GPU serving + Hindi-belt GTM.
- Milestones: 100K installs & 6K paying (M6), 500K & 30K (M18), 1M & 80K (M30).

## 9. KPIs

- North star: **doubts resolved per week** (target: 1M/week by M18)
- D7 retention ≥ 35%, free→paid conversion ≥ 6%, voice doubts share ≥ 40%,
  answer accuracy (sampled human QA) ≥ 92%, NPS ≥ 50
